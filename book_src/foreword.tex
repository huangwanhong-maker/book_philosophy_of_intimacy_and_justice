\noindent
This book is written in gratitude to one I deeply love: the girl of the forest and the mountains, who loves to travel, and loves the natural world, and above all the mountains and the forests, and who loves political economy and culture. We are natives of the same homeland, yet it was only in Japan that we met, as though by a miracle. She became the reason I wrote this series of papers and this book, and the moving force behind my building, and my living out, of its theory.

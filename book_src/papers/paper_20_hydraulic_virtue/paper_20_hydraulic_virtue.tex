The highest good is like water. Water benefits the ten thousand things\\[0.15em]

\itshape

Generating yet not possessing, acting yet not presuming,\\[0.15em]
fostering growth yet not ruling: this is called the dark virtue.\\[0.6em]
{\upshape\footnotesize\color{gold} --- Laozi, \emph{Daodejing}, ch.~51}

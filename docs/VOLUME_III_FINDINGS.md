## Paper XIX — On the Emergence of Happiness

> Unusually self-aware, with dedicated "guard/self-brake/honesty" passages; the
> [E]/[S]/[H] evidence tags in §5 are exemplary and should be preserved. The core
> (the symbolic–real gap lived in a "mode of fullness"; happiness as hermeneutic)
> is genuinely interesting and mostly self-standing. The problem is *structural
> over-building*: elaborate apparatus whose only payload is a negative point.

1. **The manifold must *constrain* meaning yet *not adjudicate* it — but
   constraint is a form of adjudication (inconsistency, central).** §2
   `gen-hermeneutic` says dynamics "cannot adjudicate" meaning; §2 `gen-manifold`
   says the neural value-signal *excludes* the reading "waiting = empty suffering"
   ("a path with no ground to stand on"). If the sub-symbolic can *exclude* a
   meaning, it does the adjudicative work the hermeneutic thesis forbids; "terrain
   not railing" is a distinction without a functional difference. **Fix:** weaken
   one pillar — dynamics constrains but does not *determine* (a spectrum), or the
   manifold excludes nothing and arbitrariness is barred only by the intersubjective
   symbolic.
2. **The van der Pol equation and all of §4 do no argumentative work — conceded,
   then reclassified as a virtue (decorative-formalism).** By the paper's own
   hermeneutic thesis the formalism *cannot* carry weight; its positive content is
   one negative claim (formal languages don't reach meaning) needing no equations.
   The "charts the shape of the manifold" reframe is itself metaphor — no actual
   manifold, metric, or curvature is specified. **Fix:** compress §4 to ~2 pages,
   keep the figure as one side-image, drop "charts the shape of the ground" unless
   a real constraint the shape imposes is exhibited.
3. **"The breadth is the proof" is an evidential fallacy (over-claim).** Six
   formal vocabularies each failing to reach meaning adds rhetorical weight, not
   logical force — if the argument is valid one instance settles it. Several
   "translations" are loose analogies dressed as identities ("The translation is
   exact"). **Fix:** delete "the breadth is the proof"; recast the table as "the
   same boundary recurs across registers"; soften "exact" to "suggestive."
4. **"Resonance, not merger" is established by relabeling, not argued
   (argument-gap).** The chapter answers its own sharp question by *naming* the
   answer ("translatable yet not exhausted," "mutual epistemic hospitality") and
   cross-referencing XIII/XVII/XVIII — a web of isomorphisms with no independent
   load-bearing step. **Fix:** supply one worked account of how remainder-leaving
   mutual translation differs *in practice* from slow merger.
5. **The "three silences" are unified by arrangement + Hegelian vocabulary, not
   argument (argument-gap).** The first two are oughts directed at the *other*; the
   third is a phenomenological state of the *relation* — calling their
   concatenation an *Aufhebung* imports a necessity that is asserted. **Fix:** argue
   the internal necessity, or downgrade to "three related figures of silence" and
   drop "completes a series."
6. **§8 "unwritability = self-verification" is circular and its own brake guts it
   (weak-section).** If *every* lived situation is unwritable (`brake2`), then this
   paper's non-exhaustion evidences nothing specific about happiness — the
   "verification" confirms only that writing ≠ life, a truism, and is exactly the
   unfalsifiable structure §2 warns against. **Fix:** present §8 as a *thematic*
   coda (the paper enacts its reticence), drop "proof."
7. **The argument is buried under scaffolding about the argument
   (redundancy/structure).** Nearly every subsection opens with meta-commentary on
   its own "load-bearing" status; the three-register isomorphism is restated 5×.
   **Fix:** strip the per-section preambles to one line; state the isomorphism once
   and back-reference.

## Paper XX — The Hydraulic Virtue (On the Ethics of Water)

> **Objective note:** cites `\textcite{wan2026braid}` but the project notes gloss
> it "Huang (2026)" — author-key/name variant worth reconciling (cf. `wan2024grb`).

1. **The four hardest objections are answered by re-labeling an ethical
   distinction in vector-calculus (decorative-formalism).** The Helmholtz
   (solenoidal/gradient) decomposition is a theorem about vector fields; that an
   intimate relation's "power force" *is* such a field, and that "circulates rather
   than sinks" *is* the good descent, is asserted by analogy. Stripped of the math,
   the answer to Nietzsche and feminism reduces to "good yielding is the kind that
   doesn't dissolve you" — what needed showing. The paper concedes "the exactness
   is what converts an evocation into an answer." **Fix:** state the ethical
   criterion (return-to-the-drained-party) in its own terms and defend it directly;
   present curl/gradient as illustration.
2. **The ethics does not stand without the forthcoming companion `wan2026braid`
   (over-claim).** All four live challenges *plus* the practical incompleteness
   result are discharged by appeal to theorems the reader can't inspect ("the
   companion proves…" 5×). **Fix:** for the two gravest challenges
   (self-dissolution, survival) supply a self-contained ethical argument that
   survives even if a reader rejects the formalism; demote the companion to
   corroboration.
3. **The licensing meta-ethics ("thin relational moral realism") is declared, not
   argued (argument-gap).** It is the hinge that turns a structural fact about
   circulation into a fact about *the good*; the paper concedes it is "a
   declaration the survey motivates rather than proves." Without it the holonomy
   criterion is "at best a precise encoding of a preference." **Fix:** argue the
   realism (the Railton naturalist reduction it gestures at is a real resource), or
   make the translation layer's claims explicitly conditional.
4. **"Structural, not cultivational" collapses into interiority in the practice
   chapter (inconsistency).** The founding boast is that a *structural* reading
   tells generative from dominative where cultivation cannot — but the operative
   tests ("retained as a debt?", "do you love him or the version you're
   cultivating?") are precisely inner dispositions invisible from outside. **Fix:**
   show these are structurally observable in the relation's flow over time, or
   soften the polemic and admit the water-good is adjudicated partly in the
   interior.
5. **"Chosen good, not formless yielding" defuses servility only retrospectively
   (over-claim).** No ex-ante mark of the lower bound is given ("good unless it
   drains you — which you discover when drained"); §4 admits the water figure works
   *against* "chosen" ("water's descent is not chosen but compelled"); and "the
   breach… the flood that overruns the bank" reintroduces the contention that
   non-contention forbids. **Fix:** give a prospective threshold (asymmetry of
   return over N cycles); reconcile "never contend" with "breach the bank when
   drained."
6. **The "five virtues vs five challenges" is an imposed schema (structure).** The
   challenges are numbered 1, 1′, 2, 2″, 3 (two are variants), collapse to two
   clusters, and two virtues face no challenge; one criterion answers all four
   "live" challenges — presented as "the strongest evidence the thesis is right,"
   but equally consistent with the criterion being too coarse (an unfalsifiability
   worry). **Fix:** adopt the honest "three contested virtues, two clusters, one
   criterion"; drop the universality-as-proof inference.

## Paper XXI — The Field of "Travel" (~123 pp)

> Genuinely integrated at the level of its reused apparatus (good/bad cycle, three
> registers, subject-preservation) — not four disconnected essays — but the
> integration is bought by heavy restatement and by subsuming everything under
> prior series theory.

1. **The "concrete universal" method is abandoned by the generalization
   (over-claim).** §7–§10 strip travel to a portable perceptual skill ("A
   wildflower at the roadside is enough"; "was never, finally, about travel") —
   the exact dissolution into "any shared field of contingency" the method was
   meant to avoid. **Fix:** defend travel as the *privileged* (not merely
   convenient) instance — its simultaneous, forced actualization of all
   field-features isn't reconstructable piecemeal — or rename "generalized travel"
   as a distinct weaker concept.
2. **Architecture is improvised (inconsistency/structure).** The abstract orders
   the movements aesthetic→ethical; the body runs ethics *before* the aesthetic
   block; and §s05b says its four criteria were "first stated" in §s06b, which the
   reader reaches *later*. **Fix:** reorder, or fix the provenance claim; reconcile
   the abstract's order with reading order.
3. **Restatement is the main length driver (redundancy).** The good/bad-cycle
   schema is re-derived in full 4×; "the decisive variable is what is done with it"
   recurs near-verbatim; the four criteria stated in full 3×; "χαλεπὰ τὰ καλά"
   closes a dozen+ subsections (a tic). **Fix:** state the criterion once, then
   *apply* in a sentence; ration the refrain.
4. **The aesthetic movement floats free of travel (weak-section).** §s06b (9.8k
   words: Hippias Major, antinomy of taste, Plotinus/Aquinas/Hegel, East Asian
   aesthetics) and §s08b (Kierkegaard's Either/Or) read as a standalone aesthetics
   treatise. **Fix:** compress to what the three-register resolution needs;
   foreground the travel-specific payoff; consider an appendix.
5. **"First statement of relational production as a domain" inflates a synthesis
   (over-claim).** The section concedes each of its four criteria "corresponds to
   one of the series' pillars" (IX, wan2024grb, XV/XVII, XVII). **Fix:** "first
   explicit *consolidation*," not "theoretical event."
6. **The "light formal rendering" asserts its load-bearing step, doesn't
   demonstrate it (unsupported-claim), and clashes with the paper's own
   anti-formalism (χαλεπὰ τὰ καλά).** "A system without diversity cannot escape a
   degenerating attractor" is stated, not shown, then hedged to "only a gesture."
   **Fix:** soften to "suggests, heuristically," or cite where the result is
   actually established.
7. **The answer to elitism is asserted and romanticized (unsupported-claim).**
   "The field's good is universally available regardless of means" sits against the
   paper's reliance on leisured connoisseurship (Bashō, gallery-going) — and its
   own Rancière "distribution of the sensible" cuts against the optimism. **Fix:**
   argue it, or downgrade to "not confined to the wealthy."

## Paper XXII — Relational Aesthetics (~138 pp, the series terminus)

> Distinctive contributions are real and locally well-made: the
> objectivist/subjectivist inference-to-best-explanation (s13); the aesthetic-
> ethical genealogy with a dissenting Nietzschean witness (s6); the falsifiable
> neuro-interface with an inter-brain-synchrony common-input control (s17); the
> political-economy self-defense (s21).

1. **Four "battlefields" run one template four times — the primary length driver
   (redundancy).** s10/s14/s15/s16 each execute an identical gather→conflicts→
   "same shape" inversion→layered synthesis→materialist-resolution schema; the
   paper concedes "the same … earned four times was, each time, the same." **Fix
   (highest-value):** state the inversion + materialist-resolution schema *once*,
   let each battlefield contribute only its distinctive traditions — cuts 25–35 pp
   with zero argumentative loss.
2. **"Unifies the series' fields" is asserted by retrospective relabeling
   (over-claim).** Poetry→reproduction, vow→reception, water→non-grasping,
   travel→perception are each assigned in a single sentence; the four-step model is
   never shown *doing work* inside a prior paper's analysis. **Fix:** re-run at
   least one prior paper's core case through the four movements, or downgrade to "a
   unifying redescription."
3. **The orthogonality guard is unreconciled with the coupling thesis
   (argument-gap).** s7 identifies the good with "the perceptible generative
   direction"; s8 says a field "may accumulate holonomy beautifully upon the
   consumption of one member" (justice not perceived). But the series' good cycle
   *is* non-extractive — so either generativity ≠ non-extraction (contra the
   series) or a truly fine sensibility *would* register the leak (collapsing the
   guard). **Fix:** distinguish *net* field holonomy from its *distribution across
   members*, and argue why fine perception of net accumulation can't register
   distributional asymmetry — or weaken to "does not automatically."
4. **"Beauty = coupling event" is mostly imported/stipulated (unsupported-claim).**
   The one self-standing argument (IBE against objectivism/subjectivism) is buried
   under a framework-derivation from the holonomy value-theory, and the underwriting
   identity is defended only by "fruitfulness," not non-circularity. **Fix:**
   foreground the IBE; mark the holonomy identification as a framework posit.
5. **The four-step model is a schema over-sold as a minimality derivation
   (argument-gap).** "Exactly these four, in this order… the smallest sequence" is
   not defended against 3- or 5-step alternatives; the worked example yields "four
   steps, five failures." **Fix:** present as a productive heuristic; drop or argue
   the minimality claim.
6. **"The form enacts the claim" treated as evidence (over-claim).** "That the four
   inversions share a structure is the paper's *strongest evidence*…" — a structure
   the author imposed can't confirm it tracks reality. **Fix:** keep one grace
   note; drop the evidential claim.
7. **Triple-statement habit (prose → `\claim` box → casebox → recap) roughly
   doubles many subsections (redundancy).** **Fix:** let the `\claim` box carry the
   compressed statement; cut the surrounding restatements.
8. **s19/s20 transpose prior results at full-section weight (weak-section).**
   **Fix:** compress each to a short corollary.

## Paper XXIII — The Aesthetic Philosophy of the Quotidian

> Mostly carefully scoped and well-architected; the perception thesis avoids naive
> idealism. The most serious issue is #1 — its signature relational move is the one
> claim its otherwise-rigorous empirical spine doesn't support.

1. **The relational half of the thesis is ungrounded (argument-gap).** The body
   grounds cultivability entirely in *individual* plasticity (early sensory cortex,
   sommelier, radiologist); the distinctive claim — perception generated *between
   two perceivers*, "what two come to perceive together, each learns to perceive" —
   has *no* citation, though hyperscanning/second-person-neuroscience/joint-
   attention entries sit *uncited* in the paper's own refs.bib. **Fix:** deploy
   them, or explicitly scope the plasticity spine to the individual pole and label
   the relational transfer a phenomenological posit awaiting evidence.
2. **Rancière is bent from politics to skill, and used as authority-citation
   (over-claim).** *Partage du sensible* is the partition of who/what is
   *perceptible and counted*, not the distribution of perceptual *skill* (the
   Marx/Bourdieu register). The genuine axis ("whether a person is perceived") is
   mentioned once then dropped. **Fix:** develop the "whether a person is
   *perceived*/counted" axis, or downgrade Rancière to one member of the political-
   economy cluster.
3. **Model I reproduces XXII's four-step cycle, and XXII is never cited
   (redundancy).** The perception→reception→sharing→reproduction skeleton is XXII's;
   the body cites paperix/paperxix but *no key for XXII anywhere*. **Fix:** cross-
   reference XXII and state what XXIII adds (quotidian scope, plasticity spine,
   distribution/justice spine).
4. **The holonomy criterion is imported and never given content here
   (unsupported-claim).** Every "goodness" verdict rests on "positive geometric
   phase" whose meaning is offloaded to other papers. **Fix:** one self-contained
   operational paragraph, or let the plainer "value returns to all who generate it"
   carry it.
5. **Enumerative scaffolding tips into cataloguing in §8, §10, §11
   (weak-section).** Three-spines, Model IV, the fourth face do real work, but the
   house style (three tiers/three pathologies/four registers ×2/four models/four
   faces/four fissures) inflates the survey sections. **Fix:** compress §8 to its
   one new move; tighten §10–§11.
6. **"Generates not registers" slogan vs the careful negotiated thesis
   (inconsistency).** §8's careful form (perception = negotiation between frame and
   sensory correction) is the defensible one; the bald slogan invites easy
   refutation. **Fix:** lead with the negotiated form; mark the slogan as shorthand.

## Paper XXIV — The Emergence of Culture

> §7 (classical-Chinese material) is deliberately left partly open by the author —
> intentional, not counted as a defect; its one asserted claim (the verbal force of
> *hua*) is appropriately hedged.

1. **"The Real given a relational form" never confronts Lacan's own thesis that the
   Real forecloses relation (argument-gap / possible category error).** The whole
   spine identifies the bond/"us" as *Real*, but Lacan's paradigm couple-statement
   is *il n'y a pas de rapport sexuel* — the Real is the *impossibility* of the
   relation. The criterion "exceeds symbolization" is also under-determined (objet
   a, sinthome, fantasy all exceed it without being the Real), and the
   justification is functional ("only a relational Real can name it"). **Fix (high
   value):** one paragraph engaging *pas de rapport sexuel* directly — argue the
   relational Real is the positivized *residue* of that impossibility (the bond as
   what circles the non-relation), which would *strengthen* the revision.
2. **"Capture-as-decoherence" borrows a term its own text contradicts
   (decorative-formalism/inconsistency).** Physical decoherence is a *transfer* of
   coherence (information leaks, not destroyed); the paper says coherence is
   *destroyed not acquired* — the opposite — and reuses "decoherence" for two
   unrelated events (capital's extraction s28; breakup s24). **Fix:** use a plain
   term ("the sign kept, the coherence lost"), or make the disanalogy explicit and
   reserve the word for one phenomenon.
3. **Pillar 4 (register configuration) is intensification-by-assertion, largely
   restating Pillars 1–2 (weak-section).** Undefended degree-claims ("thick,"
   "nakedly") with no measure; the real-anchoring thesis it carries is hedged into
   near-unfalsifiability ("not an equation but a tension" — recurs verbatim across
   theses). **Fix:** merge Pillar 4 into 1–3, or give it one discriminating
   prediction (which private forms survive a partner's death).
4. **"Four determinate revisions" of Lacan inflates the originality (over-claim).**
   By the paper's own admission Revision Two is "almost no difference," Three is an
   application; effectively one real revision. **Fix:** rename "one recovery, one
   retention, one extension, one revision."
5. **The reply to the power tradition's "no outside" is imported from Paper IX by
   citation, not argued here; the "friction" image is decorative; the point
   restated 3× (argument-gap/redundancy).** **Fix:** argue *within this paper* why a
   couple generates its own counter-power (the exit-veto of s20/s24 *is* a
   structural counter-power); compress the restatements.
6. **"Weak analogy, not structural identity" (s14) contradicts the five theses,
   all stated "for culture as such / at any scale" (inconsistency).** **Fix:** state
   which theses hold at which scale — likely mechanisms are dyad-specific, theses
   hold wherever their antecedent condition obtains.
7. **Near-verbatim duplication beyond avowed motif (redundancy).** The negation-of-
   the-negation figure stated in full 5×; the five pillars re-listed in full 3×.
   **Fix:** cut the s17 pillar-preview; state the Hegelian figure once canonically.

## Paper XXV — Optimization Theory and Rationality (the series' anti-formalism paper)

> Genuinely strong where it steelmans the opponent (satisficing, revealed
> preference, expected utility, the genetic-fallacy worry); "optimal betrayal" and
> "the audited relationship" are effective. Vulnerabilities cluster on one fault
> line: it claims a *formal, value-neutral proof* and a *clean escape from the
> optimizing form*, and delivers premise-dependent philosophy.

1. **The "formal proof… carrying no value judgment" is a chain of premise-dependent
   arguments (over-claim).** Each impossibility rests on a thick rejectable premise
   (the drive extinguished by attainment; no metalanguage; Razian incommensurability;
   the constituted subject); the hard-nosed decision theorist just denies premise 1
   and the proof never starts. **Fix:** downgrade to "a *conditional* demonstration:
   *given* the series' relational-psychoanalytic ontology, optimization is
   ill-posed"; stop claiming value-neutral compulsion.
2. **The "value-free" first movement contains an overtly normative claim — pricing
   as *betrayal* (self-undermining).** "A wrong done to them" sits inside the
   movement whose whole purpose is to be value-free — the exact leak the paper says
   the separation "is the guarantee" against. **Fix:** split §6 into a formal part
   (incommensurability ⇒ argmax undefined) and move "pricing as betrayal" into
   movement two.
3. **The scalarity linchpin ignores multi-objective/Pareto optimization
   (argument-gap).** Pareto returns a non-dominated frontier *without* a common
   scale — the standard formal response to incommensurability, and the omission a
   decision theorist seizes first. **Fix:** concede Pareto, then argue it still
   fails (a frontier needs each objective independently well-defined, which
   impossibilities 1/2/4 deny) — this *strengthens* the case.
4. **The recovered "non-optimizing" rationality uses maximizing language
   (self-undermining).** "Accord *most truly*," "the *highest* rationality," "getting
   the other right" reinstate a select-the-best structure over a non-scalar
   criterion. **Fix:** distinguish fittingness as a *threshold/responsive* concept,
   not a maximand; purge "most truly/highest."
5. **§13 crowns wuwei "the highest rationality" while §14 insists on a flat
   unsynthesizable plurality (inconsistency).** Naming one mode "highest" is a
   ranking. **Fix:** soften §13 to "the *limit case* / furthest from the maximizing
   frame."
6. **"The optimal and the good are orthogonal" over-claims independence — and
   borrows the geometric metaphor the paper distrusts (over-claim).** The argument
   shows non-identity/possible divergence, not that the optimal carries *no*
   evidential weight toward the good (much optimization of well-being does track
   it). **Fix:** "mutually irreducible / not co-extensive."
7. **Series-level: the blanket non-structurability argument threatens the series'
   *own* variational formalism (XI free-energy minimization, XIII trust-as-
   precision) yet insulates only the geometric-phase apparatus (inconsistency).**
   Free-energy minimization *is* a variational optimization applied to intimacy;
   the four impossibilities apply to it with equal force. **Fix:** one paragraph
   applying §9's "formalism's reach ≠ nature's procedure" to XI/XIII's own models
   (the move XIX already makes for the phase portrait) — otherwise the book reads as
   internally at odds. *(This connects to the series-wide formalism decision in the
   Volume I/II cross-cutting notes.)*

## Paper XXVI — The Limitation of Language

> Earns its capstone role: gathers the series' motifs (manque, generativity,
> non-possession, justice-as-guarding) under one thesis with a real spine — the §12
> "closure would extinguish" thought experiment does load-bearing work.

1. **The Mori–Zwanzig/coalgebra "co-structure" (§s18c) advances no argument and
   over-claims exactness (decorative-formalism).** By its own admission it only
   "makes the prose precise" for conclusions already reached, yet says "an exact
   result, not an analogy" — inviting the reader to conflate the theorem's rigor
   with the metaphor's fit. The algebra/coalgebra half is pure relabeling; the
   three-component choice (why three, for a dyad?) is unmotivated. **Fix:** move to
   an appendix as an avowed heuristic sketch, or make it *derive* something the
   prose can't; drop "exact result, not an analogy."
2. **"The limit is generative" slides between *persistence* and *generativity*
   (over-claim).** The §12 result is stated as *persistence* (keeping the relation
   in motion); the banner claim is *production of the new*. A perpetual circling is
   persistence without generation; only §7 argues production, near-tautologically.
   The drive/repetition material actually cuts *against* generativity. **Fix:**
   retitle around "the condition of a bond's *aliveness*," or add the step showing
   circling *generates* rather than *repeats*.
3. **Part One's six dimensions run an identical five-beat template with admitted
   near-duplicates (redundancy).** §7 concedes evolution is "the forward-facing form
   of the same generativity that justice guards"; value/justice overlap. **Fix:**
   fold justice+evolution into one "generativity, guarded backward and forward."
4. **"Drive circles, desire slides": the assigned offices go undefended
   (weak-section).** Drive=constancy / desire=movement maps too cleanly onto a folk
   stability/change binary — but repetition-compulsion (the paper's own warrant for
   the drive) is a source of *destabilizing* recurrence. **Fix:** one paragraph on
   why the drive's insistence reads as core rather than compulsion, or soften.
5. **The poem section (§16) risks recapitulating its companion Paper VIII
   (redundancy).** **Fix:** add a "what VIII established / what this adds" hinge.

## Paper XXVII — Relational Understanding

> More derivative than XXVI: its foundation (a "generative relational epistemology")
> is an imported companion recalled in §2 but never defended or cited here, and much
> recombines XXVI plus earlier papers. Original contributions (the wager §11/12; the
> seventh capacity of *preservation* §15) are strong.

1. **"Legitimacy without correctness" leaves the anti-extraction thesis floating
   (argument-gap).** The central payoff is that efficient *possession* is a
   *categorical* wrong (love vs. refined extraction) — but the paper relativizes
   even the Kantian non-instrumentalization criterion ("open to the same dialectical
   movement as everything else"), so the extractive partner can claim their own
   historically-generated legitimacy. **Fix:** separate "no final *formulation*"
   from "no stable *content*" — argue the content (the other as a non-exhausted
   co-generating subject) is invariant across reformulations.
2. **"Crossing of symbolic worlds" oscillates between metaphor and literal formal
   claim (unsupported-claim).** "Structural mapping" / "the generative rules of the
   other's world" imply a person is a codified system; the examples only deliver the
   weak hermeneutic version ("home" means different things). **Fix:** defend the
   person-as-formal-system premise, or drop the structural vocabulary for the
   hermeneutic reading the examples support.
3. **The "full crossing would end the bond" dialectic works only on the
   psychoanalytic ground it claims to do without (argument-gap).** The doctrine-free
   version treats the remainder as a *fixed store to be used up*, but the paper's own
   generativity thesis makes the other a *continuously producing* subject — so "full
   crossing" is just an unreachable asymptote (you never finish), a much tamer claim
   than "completion = death." **Fix:** concede the dramatic claim needs the Lacanian
   premise; let the generativity argument make the weaker "inexhaustible" point.
4. **§16b re-proceduralizes what §13 said could never be a procedure
   (inconsistency/weak-section).** A "kept record… day by day" with a TikZ diagram
   of a relationship-journaling instrument lands as a diary app beneath the paper's
   capstone register, and contradicts §13's anti-procedure argument. **Fix:** cut
   it, or reframe explicitly as a *non-procedural* holding-place and reconcile with
   §13.
5. **The functional case (§4–§8 + Table) states one two-move point 4× then
   tabulates it (redundancy).** **Fix:** compress two of the four functions; let the
   table carry the parallelism.

## Cross-cutting (Volume III)

**A. The two closing papers (XXVI, XXVII) dilute each other by ending identically.**
Both refuse a final synthesis, both make the "a full closing/crossing would kill the
bond" argument in nearly the same words (XXVII §18 is XXVI's death-test re-applied to
the partner), both close with a polyphonic gesture "to her." Two papers landing the
series on the same note reads as anticlimax. **Recommendation:** one paper should
*own* the death-of-closure argument and the other cite it; differentiate the two
closers' roles explicitly, or consider whether the series ends on XXVI with XXVII as
a coda.

## Paper I — Toward Relational Being in Intimate Life

1. **Load-bearing tension (inconsistency).** The case-study app is described with
   live two-party features (a shared love-journal readable by both; "xx wrote a
   new diary" notifications; a synchronous "explore together" mode), yet §7 says
   the partner is *unaware the application exists*. Those modules are unusable —
   and impermissible under §7.2's own bar on covert systems — unless she is a
   knowing participant. **Fix:** early in §6, state which modules are operative
   for the builder alone vs. built-but-dormant pending her knowing entry.
2. **Over-claim on the flagship concept (proxy epistemic injustice).** The
   calendar's "perhaps her favourite peonies" is *forbidden* as proxy epistemic
   injustice, but the near-identical witness "tip" is *permitted* — and the paper
   concedes in the same breath that the wrong "re-enters downstream." The
   distinguishing criterion leaks, weakening a headline contribution. **Fix:**
   either forbid the tip too (consistent with the calendar verdict) or sharpen
   why first-person disclosure of machine authorship neutralizes the wrong.
3. **"Lexical ordering" over-claims the method's precision.** §4 calls conflict
   resolution *lexical*, but what follows is a layered presumption mix, and §5/§8
   insist there is "no algorithm." A true lexical order is a strong, testable
   claim the paper elsewhere refuses. **Fix:** either write the strict priority
   list and defend it, or rename to "layered pluralism with absolute
   side-constraints" (which is what the text actually describes).
4. **Borrowed empirical authority (unsupported-claim).** Self-expansion,
   broaden-and-build, etc. are used to say modules *produce* relational goods
   ("not a hopeful metaphor but the predictable result"), while §8.2 concedes the
   case is a single author-designed existence-proof. The theories license the
   *design rationale*, not delivery. **Fix:** downgrade the modal verbs per
   module.
5. **Generative justice: the single-builder case is its own counterexample.**
   Presented as a clean framework "win," but the builder alone owns everything and
   the co-generator does not know the data exists — conceded only offstage in
   §8.4. Satisfies the letter (no third-party extraction), violates the spirit
   (intra-relational asymmetry). **Fix:** move the §8.4 concession forward into
   §3.13.
6. **§7 "The Absent Other" under-delivers.** Billed as the framework's severest
   test, but the hardest question — whether building a secret two-person system
   *is itself* the "managing into existence what can only be invited" the Daoist
   jurisdiction forbids — is raised in one sentence and dropped. **Fix:** expand
   §7.2 to actually adjudicate that borderline.

*Minor:* the spiking-neural-network analogy (§6.3) adds an unearned scientific
gloss; §8's tension inventory restates tensions already made in the module
subsections.

## Paper II — The Justice and Ethics of AI-Mediated Intimacy

1. **The central premise is stipulated, not argued (argument-gap).** Everything
   rides on §7.2's claim that a gesture's yield "consists in" who attended — but
   the obvious rival (recipients value a *bundle* in which authorship is one
   weighted good, not the whole yield) is never confronted. The Roxane case
   involves *total* substitution plus deception and cannot license the claim about
   partial machine-assisted noticing. **Fix:** add a subsection defending the
   inseparability premise directly, distinguishing gestures where authorship is
   plausibly the whole yield from those where it is minor.
2. **The dynamic criterion decides every hard case on a conceded-unestablished
   empirical premise (over-claim).** "Raft if it trains him out of needing it,
   fetter if into" is the sole hinge for the residual verdicts, yet §12 concedes
   the deskilling premise "is not yet established for intimate dyads." **Fix:**
   move the conditionality into the body at each verdict site ("if the deskilling
   trajectory obtains, then…"), not only into Limitations.
3. **"Convergence of independent traditions" is partly engineered (over-claim).**
   §5.2 openly concedes the antithesis was framed *to* align with the Buddhist
   objection; the "four derivations" of the dynamic criterion are one idea in four
   vocabularies. Restatement across idioms is coherence, not independent
   confirmation. **Fix:** downgrade "strongest species of evidence" to "coherence
   across registers."
4. **The legal transplant is not "doctrinally conservative" (over-claim).** Every
   anchor case (marriage, wills, the vote) is a *formal juridical act* with legal
   effect; a tender gesture has none. Extending the strictly-personal-acts
   category to informal caring cognition is an analogical transplant, not a
   conservative extension — and §4.7's own diagnosis cuts against it. **Fix:**
   reframe as a normative analogy and address the juridical-status disanalogy.
5. **The argument is fully stated three times before it is made (redundancy).**
   ~900-word abstract + §1.2 route + §1.3 "twelve contributions," then repeated
   recaps. Reads as exposition of a foregone result, in tension with the paper's
   insistence on non-finality. **Fix:** cut §1.3, compress the abstract, let §1.2
   preview questions not verdicts.
6. **The feminist "sharpest result" rests on a single study (weak-section).** The
   redistribution-vs-substitution "counterfeit currency" verdict (§10.2) rests
   entirely on Daminger 2019 and compounds premises #1 and #2. **Fix:** foreground
   the "tendencies not essences" caveat inside §10.2 and hedge "counterfeit."
7. **The eliminative table overstates (inconsistency).** §8.4 marks "Substituted
   attending — present" unconditionally; §8.3 prose says "where it occurs." The
   table is the most-quotable artifact and a reader trusts it over the prose.
   **Fix:** change to "possible / configuration-independent."

## Paper III — The Normativity of Self-Legislation

1. **The self→co-legislation move equivocates (argument-gap).** §subject
   establishes the self is constituted by *society/the symbolic order/a gendered
   order*; §regrounded needs the self constituted *by this particular beloved*.
   "That I am made a loving subject by patriarchy" does not entail "*she* holds
   co-legislative authority over our terms." The paper's positive payoff rests on
   this slide. **Fix:** add an explicit bridging premise, or lean on the
   republican/non-domination argument (which independently grounds *her* standing)
   and demote the structuralist point to "no self-certifying standpoint exists."
2. **Velleman is a hostile witness (unsupported-claim).** He is cited for
   content-responsiveness, but his love responds to the beloved's *rational
   nature*, not her empirical wants — structurally closer to the vow-lover's
   constancy. **Fix:** carry the point on Kolodny/Helm, or stage Velleman
   explicitly as a counter-case the relational theories then answer.
3. **§freewill contradicts "The Hole" (inconsistency).** §freewill claims
   reasons-responsiveness yields "a determinate result" against the vow; "The
   Hole" then concedes RR is "neutral about which reasons… and who has standing."
   The verdict smuggles in the relational premise the next subsection says RR
   lacks. Also, Fischer–Ravizza *moderate* RR is a low bar a sincere vow-lover
   clears. **Fix:** present RR as necessary-but-insufficient — it gives the
   grammar of freedom-as-responsiveness but is silent on *which* reasons, and that
   silence is what relationality fills.
4. **The "honest residue" undercuts the contestability advantage (weak-section).**
   Contestability was introduced to defeat adaptive-preference/ideological
   recognition — but the residue admits it goes *indeterminate* exactly there, the
   paradigm case it was built for. **Fix:** scope the "decisively better than
   endorsement" claim to the middle band; on deep adaptive-preference cases it is
   co-indeterminate and needs the relational-standpoint repair.
5. **Frankfurt reinterpreted without argument (inconsistency).** Cited first as
   pro-vow (volitional necessity = the shape of caring), then "properly
   understood" against the vow — substituting an object-directed Frankfurt for the
   will-centred Frankfurt of the text, undefended. **Fix:** justify the "as she
   has it" reading, or concede Frankfurt is a divided witness and let Kolodny/Helm
   carry it.
6. **The closing isomorphism over-reaches (over-claim).** "The intimate and the
   political-economic are… not analogous but identical" ignores the structural
   *coercion* (must-sell-to-survive, reserve army) that makes wage-labour
   exploitative. **Fix:** retreat to "structurally isomorphic in the distribution
   of second-order power," and address the exit/compulsion disanalogy (costly or
   foreclosed exit: children, dependence).

## Paper V — The Formation of the Subject

1. **The "layered account" partitions the tension, doesn't resolve it
   (over-claim/gap).** Assigning illusion to the image-object and veridicality to
   the world-object leaves the triadic layer's clauses (ii)–(iii) — attending to
   the other's attending, mutual openness — squarely where Lacanian méconnaissance
   operates. The paper concedes the leak ("the 'we'… is also a 'we' each party
   images"), and "JA can fail silently" *is* a triadic méconnaissance,
   contradicting "there is no structural méconnaissance." **Fix:** state the
   layering doesn't immunize the triadic layer; carry the real reconciliation in
   the *normative* ideal (presence with acknowledged gap), not an ontological
   quarantine.
2. **For *adult* intimacy the psychology is analogy, not evidence
   (argument-gap).** The developmental findings are about ontogeny; the Transfer
   Thesis is about adult *maintenance*, for which nothing cited is evidence. The
   Davidson fallback secures *formation*, not maintenance. **Fix:** lower the
   thesis to phenomenological, or add adult-appropriate evidence (attachment,
   couples-interaction, dissolution literature).
3. **"The alternative is incredible" is a false dichotomy (over-claim).** Denying
   the Transfer Thesis needn't mean the subject is *finished* — adults may be
   re-formed by *many* mechanisms (shared practice, sex, economic
   interdependence), of which joint attention is one. But the Part-Four political
   economy needs attention to be *the* mechanism (monist), which also strains the
   series' polyphonic method. **Fix:** defend the monism, or weaken to "a — the
   central — mechanism" and show the injustice claim still goes through.
4. **The fact→norm bridge is two paragraphs, and Shklar over-generates
   (weak-section).** "Justice presupposes subjects" establishes the existence of
   *some* subjects, not a duty owed to *this* person not to degrade *her*
   formation; the Shklar agency-criterion does the crossing but would make any
   arrangement-caused harm "constitutive injustice" (the foul-air factory).
   **Fix:** argue constitutive conditions carry *priority* standing (a wrong to
   the precondition of being a claimant is a distinct genus), or mark the
   institutional reach as programmatic and keep the dyadic applications as secure.
5. **"Ur-gift" vs. the bracketing of eros (scope over-claim).** Attention is
   declared "the gift presupposed by every other" while touch, bodily care, and
   sexual life — its strongest rivals for priority — are bracketed out of scope.
   **Fix:** downgrade to a *structural* limit-case claim (non-delegable,
   non-storable) and defer the priority-over-embodiment question with the erotic.
6. **The care "held-open triangle" contradicts JA's essential mutuality
   (inconsistency).** Def JA requires mutual openness; the rights/parasite
   arguments rest on that rigidity; but care is unilateral address to someone who
   can't occupy the second vertex. **Fix:** name a degenerate mode (unilateral
   tending / JA⁰) where the *formative* mechanism is suspended but the *ethical*
   posture persists — keeping clauses (ii)–(iii) rigid where the argument needs.
7. **One infant experiment (Murray–Trevarthen double-video) carries the whole
   scale-ladder (redundancy).** Deployed 5× to bridge infant → adult lover →
   dementia → algorithmic feed. **Fix:** establish once, name the extrapolation,
   then cite the generalization.

## Paper VI — Toward a Just Proposal

> Objective note: VI is an explicit "theory-core installment" that defers its
> constructive body. Two reviewers independently flag this as the paper's central
> problem *as a book chapter*.

1. **What remains is closer to a promissory note than "securing the core"
   admits (argument-gap).** The Catala mapping supplies *vocabulary* (if there is
   a wrong it is hermeneutical, self-to-self) but is verdict-neutral on whether
   the impediment *obtains*; the load-bearing claim (staging *impedes* rather than
   merely *accompanies* self-interpretation) is stated with "can"/"tends to," and
   the diagnosis itself is deferred to the body. **Fix:** pull a compact
   diagnostic argument into this installment, or make the abstract say plainly it
   establishes framework-and-fit, with the wrong's existence argued later.
2. **Consent theory (§3.1) quietly upstages the advertised Catala apparatus
   (inconsistency/over-claim).** §3.1 already delivers "falls short even with a
   sincere proposer" theory-neutrally; the paper never says what the pluralist-
   agency framing *adds* beyond relabeling impaired understanding/voluntariness.
   **Fix:** state the surplus (consent scores one answer's validity; Catala's
   self-interpretive axis names a wrong to ongoing sense-making that survives
   valid consent; empowerment-vs-domination grounds the constructive ideal), or
   foreground consent theory and demote Catala to constructive vocabulary.
3. **§3's eight-school jurisprudence tour breaks the stated two-task scope
   (structure).** §Orientation lists only verify+map; §3 runs nine subsections
   doing substantial diagnostic work (contradicting "does not yet write the
   body"), and the promised mapping is misfiled *under* §3. **Fix:** promote the
   mapping to its own top-level section (label now placed there); add §3 to the
   roadmap as a third task or split it into its own installment.
4. **"Even absent ill will" equivocates (over-claim).** It slides from "no
   malice/prejudice" (earned) to "without his doing anything" (over-stated) — but
   the constructive remedies presuppose the proposer *could and should* have
   staged otherwise. **Fix:** separate the two — no malice, but responsibility for
   the chosen form — and supply (or mark as owed) the account of how a situational
   condition constitutes an *attributable* wrong rather than bad luck.
5. **The core rests on an un-page-verified Catala monograph (unsupported-claim).**
   §2 is titled "The Field, *Verified*," yet chapter attributions and the
   "self-to-self axis" term are hedged as not-yet-sourced — exactly what a
   theory-core installment exists to secure, and what the project's
   no-fabricated-citations discipline targets. **Fix:** hard-verify against the
   2025 OUP text before the volume freezes (chapter titles, that "self-to-self" is
   Catala's own term, that Ch. 6 pairs transformative experience with
   empowerment); add page refs now.
6. **As a bound-book chapter it reads as preface to an absent work
   (incompleteness).** **Fix:** adopt the series' own solution (cf. VII,
   "*Prolegomena* to…") — reframe VI as a self-standing prolegomenon and strip the
   "installment / not yet / the full paper will" scaffolding; or write the
   constructive body and fold it in before the book is final.

## Paper IV — The Language of the Gift

1. **The impossibility result rests on a false premise (unsupported-claim).** The
   headline "context-forgetting is architectural, unrepairable" follows *only*
   because the interpretation map `h` is *defined* per-symbol — but Akerlof's
   gift-exchange is a *reciprocity schedule* (history/state-dependent), which the
   paper's own §2.2 describes as "an entry in an implicit ledger." A per-symbol
   homomorphism cannot be stateful. **Fix:** restrict the architectural claim to
   the genuinely per-symbol readings, or show how a reciprocity schedule collapses
   to per-symbol `h` (it doesn't obviously).
2. **Finite-state/context-sensitive dichotomy erases the middle (over-claim).** A
   running balance *is* state; weighted automata occupy a large auditable middle
   ground, so "unrepairable" is an artifact of choosing the *weakest* `h`. And
   Shieber (cross-serial dependencies) is about *syntax*, not meaning — borrowed
   authority. **Fix:** argue the real claim (relational meaning needs *unbounded*
   context), don't rest on a per-symbol/context-sensitive binary.
3. **The "Proposition" formalizes objects the paper says don't exist
   (weak-section).** No dynamics on `G` is defined, yet a "fixed point of the
   practice dynamics" is stated as a Proposition. **Fix:** rename to
   Observation/Conjecture, or give a toy contraction dynamics.
4. **The justice payoff `R` (recirculation depth) is a deferred sketch
   (structure).** The repair the *title* promises does no work beyond being named.
   **Fix:** one worked 3-node toy history separating extractive from generative.
5. **At 4.8k words imports are asserted (over-claim/compression).** Sharpest: the
   Waldfogel deadweight-loss = "footprint of sign-value" identification (a
   contestable empirical claim) gets one sentence. **Fix:** defer the Fricker
   strand (most severable) and defend the deadweight-loss claim, which does real
   inferential work.

**Formalism verdict:** `h` is illuminating as *notation*, over-sold as *proof* —
the "architectural/unrepairable" force is smuggled in by the per-symbol
definition.

## Paper VIII — The Intervention of Language (excursus)

> Body lives in `sections.tex` incl. the `sec:grammar` capstone.

1. **The capstone abandons the "homology not identity" discipline the series
   polices (inconsistency).** §9.4: "not analogous by coincidence; they are **one
   structure**" — the exact move §7.6 forbids for Eglash ("homology of form, not
   identity of substance… the over-unification I have refused"). It is also the
   `<!-- ai-draft -->` section. **Fix:** restate as "one *form* seen in two orders
   (knowing / language)"; the point survives as homology, losing only the
   overreach.
2. **The λ=0 label is formally wrong on the paper's own terms (inconsistency).**
   λ=0 means *extinction* (no output); the pseudo-lyric *does* output sterilely —
   that's the metronome (λ≈1, repetition of the identical) in the paper's own
   framework. The one discriminating use of the borrowed math mislabels it.
   **Fix:** pseudo-lyric = λ=1, or drop the numeric dressing.
3. **The generative-grammar apparatus is decorative (decorative-formalism).**
   §9.1–9.2 recast §7.7's already-complete prose point ("generator not container")
   in λ>1 / "phase increment" notation with no grammar, no productions, nothing
   counted. **Fix:** supply a minimal toy production system, or frame λ/phase
   openly as metaphor.
4. **The case study is unfalsifiable by construction (over-claim).** Poems are
   sorted *by* illocutionary mechanism, and metaphor/metonymy are asserted
   exhaustive — so no poem *can* fail to map; "test/verification" is circular.
   **Fix:** state a genuine falsification condition, or downgrade to "illustration."
5. **"No-thesis" method belied by the abstract (structure).** The abstract already
   announces spine and conclusion. **Fix:** soften to "defers *argued* commitment."

**Formalism verdict:** the generative-grammar analogy is *largely decorative*; its
real value is as a *homology* (method mirrors object) — precisely what "one
structure, not by coincidence" overstates into identity.

**Redundancy IV↔VIII:** Lacan (need/demand/desire, "giving what one does not
have," *objet a*) is expounded from scratch in *both*; Eglash's generative justice
/ recirculation-vs-extraction is re-derived in VIII §7.6 rather than citing IV;
gift-as-non-exchangeable-surplus appears in both vocabularies. **Fix:** let one
paper carry each shared exposition and have the other cite it.

## Paper VII — Prolegomena to a Diplomacy

1. **The holonomy/"Value Foam" formalism fails on two technical points
   (over-claim/gap) — priority item.** (a) *Wrong locus:* holonomy is a property
   of the loop and connection, not of who walks it; both parties on the same
   circuit γ under the same connection get the *same* Hol(γ), so "same circuit,
   different deformation per party" is exactly what one connection cannot give —
   the asymmetry is smuggled into the undefined phrase "the connection itself is
   asymmetric." (b) *Wrong object:* holonomy returns a group element
   (non-commutativity of transport), **not** the non-conservation of a scalar;
   "value leaking to the stronger party" is a conservation-law failure needing a
   non-closed 1-form / source term, not a Berry phase. **Fix:** demote to an
   analogy for path-dependence/irreversibility only (which it legitimately
   captures), or name the correct object (a cohomological obstruction); stop
   saying the formalism "states it exactly" while conceding the demonstration is
   deferred.
2. **The coupling matrix presupposes what it claims to display
   (structure/gap).** "The matrix *shows* this; it does not *presuppose* it… the
   visual refutation of the hub" — but the cells are hand-assigned and the author
   flags them "indicative"; the gift's row is all-strong *by construction*, so the
   anti-hub conclusion is circular. **Fix:** reframe the matrix as a
   conjecture-map and rest the anti-hub case on the conceptual arguments of §2.
3. **Two of the three "distinctive contributions" are the deferred ones
   (incompleteness).** Only the re-description is delivered here; holonomy and the
   computational chain are promissory yet written up as achievements. **Fix:** one
   honest sentence separating what is *established* from what is *staked out*.
4. **"Value Foam" is a dangling reference (unsupported-claim).** Cited as both
   prior work "this series" *and* "the dedicated paper" still to come — a source a
   reader cannot consult, and it carries the paper's most distinctive formal
   claim. **Fix:** cite a real key or state plainly it is introduced here and
   formalized later — not both.
5. **The neuroscience "not a metaphor" verdict is asserted (over-claim).** Its
   three "predictions" are untested and not distinctively Bayesian ("errors
   cluster where signs are noisiest" is what *any* inference account predicts).
   **Fix:** soften to "would predict, if formalized."
6. **The "twelve frameworks" count is internally miscounted (inconsistency).** Two
   §3 subsections are both "the eighth framework"; the ordinals never reach twelve.
   Phenomenology was split from psychoanalysis late and never folded into the
   coupling account (§3.13 still pairs them). **Fix:** renumber, and specify what
   phenomenology couples to that psychoanalysis does not.
7. **Leans on uncited private companion work (self-standing).** POMDP/NeSy/Value
   Foam/NCNP references point to the author's other unshared projects. **Fix:**
   fine for book use; for any standalone release gate them behind "reported in
   [forthcoming]."

## Paper IX — The Sustainability of Intimacy

> The paper is unusually self-aware and pre-concedes most objections. The
> question is therefore not "does it hide its weakness" (it doesn't) but "does the
> argument survive the weaknesses it discloses."

1. **The "structural isomorphism" is downgraded by the paper's own appendix to an
   unproven conjecture — and the geometry does no deductive work (over-claim, the
   central issue).** §4: "not a metaphor but a structural isomorphism"; Appendix:
   "a conjecture with strong motivation and no proof… none of this is discharged
   here." The decisive tell: **strike the appendix and every "holonomy," and no
   argument fails** — non-possession, the pure-fuel criterion, poeticity⊥justice
   are all re-derived in the hermeneutic/psychoanalytic/political-economic
   registers. An admitted "conjecture with no proof" is a hypothesis, not the
   "axiom" the abstract advertises. **Fix:** pick a lane — demote the geometry to
   an explicitly *regulative analogy* everywhere (incl. abstract and the §4 "not a
   metaphor" sentence), or reframe so the argued theses headline and the bundle is
   their notation.
2. **§8 "discharges the two debts"; the appendix says they are *not* discharged
   (inconsistency).** Opposite verdicts on the paper's two most important
   self-imposed obligations. **Fix:** in §8 replace "discharges" with "identifies
   the bearer and gives the correspondence content, leaving formal verification to
   the appendix"; reserve "discharge" for the formal claim the appendix declines.
3. **Circle-vs-spiral is asserted by definitional alignment, and misdescribes the
   paper's own model (argument-gap).** "Spiral" is just the geometric name for the
   already-defined "return-with-remainder" — the very move the paper's own caveat
   forbids. And in the holonomy picture *both* cycles are base-space circles
   distinguished only by fibre; the ascending-plane-spiral (never returns) is a
   *different* geometry the text conflates. **Fix:** call circle/spiral an
   illustrative naming, and commit to one geometry (base-circle + fibre remainder,
   *or* ascending spiral).
4. **The "burden-of-proof reversal" rests on a contestable default (unsupported).**
   "Natural dynamics default to the good" is supported by two anecdotes (wounds
   heal, bodies float) — but wounds infect and bodies sink; entropy is as good a
   default, and the paper elsewhere grants young bonds must be *built*. A hedged
   premise can't carry an unhedged reversal. **Fix:** restrict to systems
   independently shown to have positive default curvature, or reframe as a
   defeasible corrective against the managerial disposition.
5. **The Daoist return-to-root is welded to the spiral while suppressing the line
   that contradicts it (argument-gap).** §4 quotes 归根 (return to root) as
   remainder-bearing, but the next unquoted clause is 归根曰静 — "returning to the
   root is *stillness*," which in the paper's own coding is the *zero-holonomy dead
   circle*, not the positive spiral. The Diamond Sutra's 是名世界 fits the
   remainder structure; 归根 arguably supports the opposite. **Fix:** argue 归根
   against 归根曰静 explicitly, or lean the remainder figure on the Diamond Sutra
   and let the Daoist material carry the *wu wei*/non-possession load it genuinely
   carries.
6. **"The form enacts the content" treats compositional choices as corroboration
   (over-claim).** That four frameworks converge, or the essay returns bearing a
   remainder, is a *design decision*, not evidence. **Fix:** keep as illustration,
   drop "corroboration."
7. **The headline formal payload is deferred in full to an unwritten sequel
   (structure).** Bearer circle-structure, additivity, gauge-invariance, the
   sublimation-proof, the branching-process λ — all handed to "the Value-Foam
   study." **Fix:** bring one minimal worked micro-example of a relational quantity
   with genuine S¹/additive structure into the appendix, or reweight the framing.

## Cross-cutting (series-level) observations

**A. The geometric-phase / holonomy formalism is the series' signature and its
single biggest exposure.** It appears as metaphor in VII, VIII, and IX (and runs
through Volumes II–III). Three independent close reads reached the same verdict:
it is an *evocative, honest analogy* that is repeatedly given the surface grammar
of a theorem ("isomorphism," "states it exactly," "axiom"). Two specific technical
problems recur and should be settled *once*, series-wide:
  - Holonomy returns a **group element** (path-dependence / non-commutativity),
    not the **non-conservation of a scalar** — so "value leaking around a loop"
    (VII, IX) is modelled by the one object that does not model it. If value-flux
    is the intended content, the right object is a non-closed 1-form / cohomological
    obstruction, not a Berry phase.
  - "Same circuit, different outcome per party" (VII) cannot come from a single
    connection; it needs the two parties in different fibres/representations,
    which is never specified.
  Recommended series-level decision: adopt one consistent stance — either the
  formalism is *regulative notation* (say so uniformly and drop "isomorphism/
  axiom/states-it-exactly"), or a genuine model (then supply the missing
  fibre/representation structure and the correct value-flux object in the promised
  "Value Foam" paper, and cite that paper with a real key). The papers' own candor
  already leans to the first; aligning the framing would cost nothing argued and
  remove the biggest target a hostile reviewer has.

**B. "The form enacts the content" as corroboration** recurs (VIII, IX, and the
series' polyphonic self-description). It is rhetorically lovely but should be
marked as illustration, not evidence, wherever it appears.

**C. Redundant from-scratch expositions** of the same sources across papers
(Lacan's desire/objet a in IV and VIII; Eglash's generative justice in IV, VIII,
and IX). In a *book*, one paper should carry each and the others cite it — this is
both a length saving and a coherence gain.

**D. Deferred cores presented as achievements.** VI, VII, and IX each defer their
formal/constructive payload to unwritten sequels while writing them up in the
indicative. In a bound book this reads as chapters that are prefaces to absent
works. Decide per paper: fold the sequel in, reframe as an avowed prolegomenon
(the VII solution), or clearly mark established-vs-staked-out.
